## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for the travel and discovery sector, emphasizing exploration, reliability, and modern efficiency. The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern** with a touch of **Glassmorphism**, creating a bridge between high-utility SaaS platforms and evocative, lifestyle-driven media.

The visual narrative relies on high-quality environmental photography contrasted with clean, structured UI elements. By utilizing semi-transparent "glass" containers for search components and crisp white cards for data, the system evokes a sense of clarity and professionalism. The emotional response is intended to be optimistic and adventurous yet grounded in a trustworthy, secure framework.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored by a vibrant **Electric Cerulean** primary color, used strategically for action-oriented elements like primary buttons, iconography, and status chips to draw immediate user attention. 

The secondary color is a deep **Midnight Navy**, providing a sophisticated contrast for typography, navigation bars, and footer elements, ensuring high readability and a professional "Enterprise" feel. Backgrounds primarily use a clean white or a soft, cool-tinted light gray to maintain an airy, expansive atmosphere reminiscent of the sky and horizon.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to achieve a friendly, contemporary, and highly legible interface. The type scale is optimized for information density and marketing impact.

Headlines use heavy weights (Bold to ExtraBold) with tight letter-spacing to create a strong visual hierarchy. Body text is kept clean and rhythmic with generous line-heights to ensure readability during longer browsing sessions. Label styles utilize semi-bold weights and occasional uppercase transforms to differentiate metadata from descriptive content.
