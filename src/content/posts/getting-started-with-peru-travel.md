Peru is one of the most diverse and captivating destinations in South America. Whether you're interested in ancient history, adventure sports, or cultural immersion, Peru has something for everyone.

## Best Time to Visit

The dry season (May to September) is generally considered the best time to visit Peru, with clear skies and comfortable temperatures. However, each season offers unique experiences:

- **Dry Season**: Perfect for hiking and outdoor activities
- **Wet Season**: Lush landscapes and fewer crowds
- **Shoulder Seasons**: Pleasant weather with moderate crowds

## Must-See Destinations

### Machu Picchu
The iconic Inca citadel is Peru's most famous attraction. Plan for at least 2-3 days to properly experience the site and surrounding area.

### Sacred Valley
Home to traditional Andean communities, market towns, and agricultural terraces. An excellent alternative to the Machu Picchu crowds.

### Lima
Peru's vibrant capital offers world-class cuisine, museums, and nightlife. Spend at least 2 days exploring the city.

### Amazon Rainforest
Experience the incredible biodiversity of the Amazon with guided jungle lodge experiences or river cruises.

### Lake Titicaca
The world's highest navigable lake offers unique cultural experiences with indigenous communities.

## Practical Tips

- **Currency**: Peruvian Nuevo Sol (PEN). ATMs are widely available in major cities
- **Language**: Spanish is the primary language. English is spoken in tourist areas
- **Transportation**: Domestic flights, buses, and trains connect major destinations
- **Accommodation**: Options range from budget hostels to luxury eco-lodges
- **Health**: Standard vaccines recommended. Travel insurance advised

## Getting to Peru

Most international visitors fly into Jorge Chávez International Airport in Lima. From there, domestic flights quickly connect you to other major destinations like Cusco (for Machu Picchu) and Puerto Maldonado (for Amazon access).
