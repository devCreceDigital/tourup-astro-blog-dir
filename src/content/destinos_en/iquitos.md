## Quick summary: why travel to Iquitos

Iquitos is the gateway to the Peruvian Amazon, the most biodiverse ecosystem on the planet. It is the largest city in the world without road access, making it a cultural and geographical island in the middle of the Amazon rainforest. From here you access eco-lodges, nature reserves, and indigenous communities that offer an authentic jungle experience.

It is the ideal destination for nature lovers, wildlife observation, wildlife photography, and those seeking to disconnect from the modern world in one of the most remote and fascinating environments on the planet.

## When is the best time to visit Iquitos

### High, shoulder, and low season

The **high season** (June to October) corresponds to the dry season. River levels drop, trails are more passable, and there are fewer mosquitoes. It is the best time for jungle hikes and land excursions.

The **shoulder season** (April, May, and November) offers a balance between rain and dry days. Water levels begin to change, allowing both hiking and channel navigation.

The **rainy season** (December to March) floods extensive areas of the jungle, creating navigable channels that allow access to the "flooded forest" (igapó). It is the best time to navigate among trees and see dolphins and fish in their natural habitat.

### Monthly weather and packing recommendations

The climate is hot and humid year-round: 82-91 °F (28-33 °C) with 80-90 % humidity. Rain is frequent but usually in the form of intense downpours lasting 1-2 hours. Pack quick-dry light clothing (cotton or synthetic materials), long-sleeved shirts for sun and insect protection, lightweight long pants, closed shoes for hiking, rain boots or waterproof shoes, insect repellent with DEET, sunscreen, wide-brimmed hat, and a headlamp.

## What to see and do in Iquitos

### Must-sees

The **city of Iquitos**: the Tarapacá waterfront on the Itaya River, the Plaza de Armas with the iron Cathedral designed by Eiffel, the Belén neighborhood (the "floating market"), the Iron House, the Museum of Indigenous Amazonian Cultures. **Amazon lodges**: 3-5 day experiences with guided excursions, night walks, wildlife spotting, and navigation on rivers and creeks. **Allpahuayo Mishana National Reserve** and **Pacaya Samiria Reserve** (the largest in Peru, only accessible by boat).

### Excursions from Iquitos

Navigation on the Amazon River to spot pink and gray river dolphins. Visit to riverside communities and animal rescue centers (such as the Amazon Rescue Center - CREA, for manatees). Night walks to see insects, frogs, and tarantulas. Piranha fishing. Birdwatching (macaws, toucans, hoatzins). Excursion to the flooded forest during the rainy season.

## How many days you need in Iquitos

### Short experience (3 days/2 nights)

Day 1: arrival in Iquitos, transfer to lodge, introductory jungle walk, night excursion. Day 2: river navigation, dolphin spotting, riverside community visit, piranha fishing. Day 3: morning walk, return to Iquitos, quick city tour, return flight.

### Complete experience (4-5 days/3-4 nights)

Add visits to Pacaya Samiria or Allpahuayo Mishana Reserve. Include longer boat excursions, birdwatching at dawn, and kayak expeditions on secondary channels. Include an extra night in Iquitos to explore the city.

### Deep experience (7+ days)

Combine two lodges in different ecosystems (Black River and Amazon River). Include multi-day boat expeditions with camping on river beaches. Participate in conservation or scientific research projects.

## Estimated budget for traveling to Iquitos

### Sample budget per person

**Budget (40-70 USD/day)**: basic lodge with shared room (3 days/2 nights), meals included, group excursions.

**Standard (100-180 USD/day)**: lodge with private room, hot water bathroom, private or small group excursions, bilingual guide.

**Premium (from 250 USD/day)**: luxury lodge with suite, customized expeditions, specialized naturalist guide, private boat navigation, specialized birdwatching.

## Health and safety tips

### Health in the Amazon

The yellow fever vaccine is highly recommended (must be applied at least 10 days before). Use insect repellent with 30 % or more DEET, especially at dusk and in shaded areas. Use a mosquito net on your bed if your lodge does not have air conditioning. Drink only bottled or filtered water. Food at lodges is safe and prepared for tourists. Bring a basic first aid kit with antidiarrheals, antihistamines, pain relievers, and bandages.

### Safety

Iquitos center is safe during the day. Avoid the Belén neighborhood (especially the floating part) without an authorized local guide. Do not walk alone at night in non-tourist areas. Inside the lodges, security is total and guides are trained to handle any situation in the jungle. Always follow the guide's instructions on excursions.

## Best tours and experiences in Iquitos

The main experience in Iquitos is the **Amazon lodge with all-inclusive package**: airport-lodge transfer, meals, guided excursions, and activities. Lodges range from basic (Mosquito Azul, Amazon Mermaid) to luxury (Ceiba Tops, Treehouse Lodge). The **Iquitos city tour** includes Plaza de Armas, the waterfront, Belén market, and the Iron House. For the more adventurous, **multi-day expeditions to Pacaya Samiria Reserve** offer a total immersion experience in the Amazon rainforest.
