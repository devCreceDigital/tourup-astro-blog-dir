## Quick summary: why travel to Arequipa

Arequipa is not just a beautiful colonial city: it is the heart of a region where snow-capped volcanoes exceed 20,000 ft (6,000 m), the deepest canyon in the world hosts condors in flight, and spicy food is cultural heritage. Its historic center, built from sillar (white volcanic stone), is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

It is the ideal destination for travelers seeking colonial architecture, extreme nature, and high-altitude cuisine. It combines perfectly with Cusco and southern Peru on a 10-14 day itinerary.

## When is the best time to visit Arequipa

### High, shoulder, and low season

The **high season** (May to October) offers dry and stable weather, ideal for the Colca Canyon and trekking. July and August are the busiest months. It is the best time to see the condor flight at dawn.

The **shoulder season** (April and November) offers good weather with fewer tourists. September and October are excellent for combining Arequipa with Colca without crowds.

The **low season** (December to March) has sporadic rainfall, especially in Colca (January-February are the rainiest). The advantages: fewer tourists, lower prices, and greener landscapes.

### Monthly weather and packing recommendations

Arequipa has a temperate and dry climate. Daytime temperatures range between 68-77 °F (20-25 °C) year-round. Nights are cold (46-54 °F / 8-12 °C) from May to September. Pack light clothing for the day, a jacket for the night, hiking shoes, high-factor sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat for Colca excursions.

## What to see and do in Arequipa

### City must-sees

The **Plaza de Armas** with the white sillar Cathedral and colonial portals. The **Santa Catalina Monastery**, a city within a city with cloisters, streets, and squares. **Casa del Moral** and **Casa Tristán del Pozo**, colonial mansions with sillar courtyards. **Carmen Alto viewpoint** with panoramic views of the city and volcanoes. The **Andean Sanctuaries Museum** with the Juanita mummy, the Inca maiden sacrificed on Ampato.

### Nearby excursions

The **Colca Canyon** (2 days), one of the deepest canyons in the world, with **Cruz del Cóndor** as the main spot for viewing the condor flight at dawn. The **Valley of the Volcanoes** in Andagua, with over 80 volcanic cones. **Cotahuasi Canyon**, even deeper than Colca and less visited. The **La Calera hot springs** in Chivay. The **sillar route** to see volcanic stone quarries.

## How many days you need in Arequipa

### Quick stop (2 days)

Day 1: city tour (Plaza de Armas, Santa Catalina, viewpoints) and Juanita museum. Day 2: express Colca Canyon excursion (early departure, Cruz del Cóndor visit, return).

### Classic trip (3-4 days)

Day 1: full city tour. Day 2: travel to Chivay, afternoon at hot springs. Day 3: sunrise at Cruz del Cóndor, return to Arequipa visiting Colca villages. Day 4: Valley of the Volcanoes or food tour.

### Extended itinerary (5-7 days)

Add 2-3 day trekking in the Colca Canyon (canyon floor route with camping). Combine with Cotahuasi or the canyon route to Cusco passing through Chivay, Sibayo, and Espinar.

## Altitude, safety, and health tips

### Altitude and acclimatization

Arequipa (7,661 ft / 2,335 m) does not pose an altitude sickness risk for most. But the Colca Canyon reaches 16,076 ft (4,900 m) at its highest point. Take 1-2 days in Arequipa before heading up to Colca. Bring coca leaves, stay well hydrated, and avoid alcohol the night before the excursion.

### Safe areas

Arequipa's historic center is safe to walk during the day. The streets around Plaza de Armas and Santa Catalina Monastery have good tourist traffic. At night, stick to the center and use ride-hailing apps. Colca tours include transportation and a guide, eliminating mobility risks.

## Best tours and experiences in Arequipa

The **2-day/1-night Colca Canyon tour** is the most popular experience, with a visit to Cruz del Cóndor, colonial villages, pre-Inca terraces, and hot springs. **City tours** cover the historic center, Santa Catalina Monastery, and the Juanita Museum. For adventurers, the **Misti ascent** (2 days) and **Cotahuasi trekking** offer unique mountain experiences. **Food tours** include Arequipeño spicy cooking classes and visits to traditional picanterías.
